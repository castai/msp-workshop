Online Boutique has been deployed.

The frontend is exposed via a LoadBalancer Service named:
  {{ .Release.Name }}-frontend
in namespace:
  {{ .Release.Namespace }}

Get the external URL with:
  kubectl get svc {{ .Release.Name }}-frontend -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} -o jsonpath='{.status.loadBalancer.ingress[0].hostname}'

The in-cluster URL is:
  http://frontend.{{ .Release.Namespace }}.svc.cluster.local
